# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect, regardless of background or experience. Critique code and ideas with specific, constructive feedback. Help beginners reproduce problems and find the right place to contribute.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual content directed at others, publishing private information, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces, including interactions representing this project.

Report problems privately to the maintainer at **mevlutbasaran01@gmail.com**. Include relevant links and context; avoid sharing private information publicly. Reports will be reviewed confidentially where possible. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Good-faith reports and constructive disagreement are welcome.
